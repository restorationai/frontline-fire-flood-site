## Restoration emergencies common in Fife Heights

Fife Heights sits in the Puget Sound lowlands, where the marine climate means long stretches of steady, saturating rain rather than short violent storms. That pattern is hard on roofs, gutters, and grading that were fine in August and overwhelmed by November. Properties near low-lying drainage paths or older stormwater infrastructure see water pond against foundations and work its way into crawlspaces, and heavy wind events off the Sound bring down limbs that puncture roofing and siding. Winter cold snaps are infrequent here but sharp when they hit, which is exactly when older galvanized or copper supply lines crack and go unnoticed until a ceiling stain or a warm floor gives it away.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Fife Heights

Much of the residential building stock in this part of Pierce County dates from the postwar decades through the 1990s, a mix of wood-frame construction on crawlspaces and slab-on-grade, with copper and later PEX plumbing replacing earlier galvanized runs as homes were updated. Older sections may still carry galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, both of which are common failure points once they pass fifty years in service, and any pre-1980s finishes should be treated as a possible source of asbestos-containing material or lead paint until tested.
