## Why Ketron Island Properties See Water Damage Issues

Ketron Island sits in Puget Sound with no bridge connection, which shapes almost everything about how its homes are built and maintained. Many structures are older cabins or seasonal residences built for weekend or summer use, with crawlspace foundations, minimal insulation, and plumbing that was never upgraded with the same frequency as mainland homes because every repair requires a special trip. Private wells and septic systems are common on the island, which means a supply line failure or septic backup carries a different risk profile than a typical municipal-water loss, since groundwater and drain field saturation can complicate both the cleanup and what needs testing afterward. Winter storms off the Sound also drive wind-blown rain into older window flashing and roof penetrations that may have gone years between inspections simply because getting a contractor out there isn't a quick errand.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process Calibrated for Island Access

The extraction and drying steps themselves follow the same IICRC S500-based standard we use anywhere: we extract standing water, measure moisture in framing and subfloor with meters rather than guesswork, and set containment and air movement based on what the readings show rather than a fixed timeline. What changes on Ketron Island is the staging. We load trucks with everything a job is likely to need, generators, extra desiccant dehumidifiers, additional air movers, and backup fuel, because a forgotten piece of equipment means a ferry wait, not a quick drive back to the shop. On properties without reliable grid power, we run drying equipment off generators and check in more frequently, since a power interruption on an island job can undo two days of drying progress overnight.

## Reaching Ketron Island from Lakewood

Getting a crew to Ketron Island means routing through Steilacoom to the Pierce County ferry dock rather than a straight drive. The island is served by a limited daily ferry schedule, which means timing matters: a call that comes in near the last run of the day changes how we stage the job and what we bring on the first trip versus what follows. We coordinate directly with property owners and caretakers on ferry timing so equipment, not just people, makes it over on the right run, and we plan return trips for continued monitoring rather than assuming we can simply swing back whenever convenient.

## Insurance Coordination for Ketron Island Properties

Waterfront and island properties often carry different coverage structures than inland homes, sometimes including separate flood policies alongside a standard homeowner's policy. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings in a format carriers can use, and we note the source of water clearly, since a wind-driven rain intrusion, a plumbing failure, and a storm surge event can trigger different claim paths. If a septic or well system is involved, we flag that early so the adjuster and any required testing can be scheduled without delaying the drying work that needs to start right away.
