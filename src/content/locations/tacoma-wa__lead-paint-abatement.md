Lead paint abatement in Tacoma almost always comes down to one number: the age of the house. With so much of the city's housing stock built before the federal 1978 lead paint ban, and a large share of that dating to the Craftsman and post-war boom years, a simple window replacement or kitchen remodel can turn into a lead-safe compliance project the moment a contractor starts scraping trim or opening a wall.

## Why Tacoma Properties See Lead Paint Issues

Tacoma's older neighborhoods, especially the bungalows and foursquares built in the first half of the twentieth century, were finished with lead-based paint as a matter of course, layered under decades of repainting. Pierce County's damp, marine-influenced climate accelerates the problem: moisture gets behind siding and window casings, paint blisters and chalks, and lead dust ends up in soil, gutters, and window wells long before anyone notices peeling on the surface. Renovation work, pressure washing, and even routine exterior painting can disturb that layered paint and release dust that settles into HVAC systems or tracks indoors on shoes and pets.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Tacoma

We start with EPA RRP-compliant testing to confirm lead content and map which components (trim, siding, interior casework) are affected before any disturbance begins. Work areas get sealed with critical barriers and negative air where applicable, components are wet-misted and removed or stabilized using lead-safe methods, and HEPA vacuuming follows every step rather than waiting until the end of the job. Because older Tacoma homes frequently have balloon-frame construction and original plaster, we pay close attention to where dust can migrate through wall cavities and floor penetrations that a newer stick-built home simply doesn't have.

## Tacoma Insurance & HOA Coordination

Lead abatement is rarely a standard insurance claim unless it's tied to a covered event, such as fire or water damage that exposes painted surfaces needing remediation before reconstruction can start. When that's the case, we document the lead findings alongside the primary damage so the paperwork supports both scopes of work. For properties in historic districts or under homeowners association review, we coordinate exterior work timing and disposal logistics so repainting and siding replacement meet both lead-safe requirements and any architectural approval process already in place.

## Local note

One detail that catches first-time renovators off guard in Tacoma: paint testing results can vary room to room, even within the same house, because additions and partial remodels over the decades introduced newer, lead-free materials next to original painted trim. We test by component, not by assumption, so a 1920s dining room and a 1990s add-on kitchen in the same house don't get treated the same way.
