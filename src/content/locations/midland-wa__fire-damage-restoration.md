## Local note

One thing that catches people off guard in this part of Pierce County: a fire contained to one room doesn't mean the smoke damage is contained too. Homes with forced-air heating pull smoke particulate through the duct system during the fire itself, so we check and often clean or seal ductwork even when the furnace room was nowhere near the flames. Skipping that step is one of the most common reasons homeowners report lingering smoke odor weeks after a restoration job elsewhere was called finished.
